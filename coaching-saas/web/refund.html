Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated:

Plans

Paid plans are bought in advance for one month or one year and start immediately after payment. There is no automatic renewal: nothing is charged again unless you buy again.

Cancellation

You can stop using a paid plan at any time; it simply ends on its end date and the institute moves to the Free plan. We do not refund the unused part of a period.

Refunds

  • If you were charged but the plan was not applied, or you were charged twice for the same order, write to us within 7 days with the order number. We will apply the plan or refund the extra payment.
  • If you bought a plan by mistake and have not used its paid features, write to us within 48 hours of payment for a full refund of the plan price.
  • Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5-7 working days. The payment-gateway charge is not refundable.
