Privacy policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what data the BatchPilot app and website collect, why, and your choices. The Service is provided by .

Who decides what is stored

BatchPilot is used by coaching institutes. Each institute decides which staff and students to add and what to record about them. For that data the institute is responsible; we store and process it on the institute's behalf.

Data we store

  • Account data: name, mobile number, username, e-mail (optional), address (optional), role (owner, sub-admin, teacher, student). Owners who sign in with Google: name and e-mail from Google.
  • Institute data: batches, timetable, attendance, homework, notices, exams and marks, fee records and receipts, study material links, doubts and photos attached to them, leave requests, accounts entries (depending on the plan).
  • Device data: a push-notification token for sending notifications to your phone.
  • Payment data for plans: order number, plan, amount, status, and the mobile number given at checkout. Card / UPI / bank details are handled by Cashfree Payments and are not stored by us.

Passwords

Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by Google Firebase Authentication. Nobody, including the institute and us, can read them; they can only be reset.

How we use data

Only to provide the Service: showing each person the information of their institute, sending notifications the institute triggers, applying plans, preventing misuse, and support. We do not sell data and do not show advertising.

Where data is kept and who can see it

Data is stored on Google Cloud / Firebase (Firestore in the Mumbai, India region). Each institute's data is separated: users can only reach data of their own institute, and inside it only what their role allows (for example a student sees their own attendance, marks and fees). Service providers we use: Google (Firebase hosting, database, authentication, notifications) and Cashfree Payments (plan payments).

Children

Student accounts are created by the institute, not by the student. The institute is responsible for having the consent of a parent or guardian for students under 18. The app is not meant to be downloaded by children on their own.

How long we keep data

As long as the institute uses the Service. When an account or institute is deleted, its data is removed from our database (backups expire within 30 days). Records of plan payments are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your choices

You can ask your institute to correct your data. You can delete your own account in the app (menu > Delete my account) or on our Delete account page. For any other request write to .

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Access is controlled by server-side rules for every request. Money records keep a tamper-proof activity log.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date.
