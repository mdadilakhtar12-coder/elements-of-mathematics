Delete your account

You can delete your BatchPilot account here or in the app (menu > Delete my account).

  • Teacher, student or sub-admin: your login and profile are deleted. Records the institute keeps about classes and fees (for example past attendance) may stay with your institute.
  • Institute owner: the whole institute is deleted: all staff and student logins and all its data. This cannot be undone.
  • Records of plan payments are kept only as long as tax law requires.
