## Full description
BatchPilot is a simple app for offline coaching and tuition institutes. The owner, teachers and students use one app; each institute's data is private and separate.

FOR THE INSTITUTE OWNER
• Batches, classes, groups and sections the way your institute works
• Students and teachers in minutes: add one by one or upload an Excel sheet
• Attendance marked by teachers class by class, approved by you
• Fees month by month, PDF receipts, your own UPI QR, daily collection report
• Timetable: weekly or daily, upload Excel / photo / PDF, send it to teachers and batches on WhatsApp
• Announcements, homework, exams, results and ranking
• Accounts, teacher salary and leave (higher plans)
• Make a teacher a sub-admin with exactly the rights you choose

FOR TEACHERS
• Today's classes, attendance in a few taps, homework and marks

FOR STUDENTS
• Timetable, attendance, homework, results and fees in one place

Start free with up to 30 students and 5 teachers. Paid plans start at Rs 99 per month.

## App access (for the Google reviewer)
Login is required. Give the reviewer a working account:
1. In your own test institute create a **sub-admin** with all permissions (or a teacher), e.g. username `playreview`, password of your choice.
2. In Play Console → App content → App access → "All or some functionality is restricted" → add:
   Institute code: `<your test institute code>` · Username: `playreview` · Password: `<password>` ·
   Instructions: "Open the app, choose Staff / Student, enter the institute code, username and password."

## Data safety (answers)
- Data collected: **Name, Email address (optional), Phone number, Physical address (optional), User IDs**; **App activity → other user-generated content** (attendance, homework, marks, fees); **Photos** (only if a student attaches one to a doubt, or a timetable photo which is read on the phone and not uploaded); **Device or other IDs** (push token).
- Purpose: **App functionality, Account management**. Not shared with third parties (Google Firebase and Cashfree act as service providers). No data sold. No ads.
- Encrypted in transit: **Yes**. Users can request deletion: **Yes** (in app + delete-account page).

## Target audience & content
- Target age group: **18 and over** (the institute owner and staff install and manage it; student accounts are created by the institute). Not designed for children.
- Content rating questionnaire: Education/utility, no violence, no user-to-user public sharing, no gambling → expected rating "Everyone / 3+".
- Government app: No. Financial features: No (plan payments are on the website, not in the app). Health: No.
