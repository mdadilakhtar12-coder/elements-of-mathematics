What happens when a visitor clicks an Enroll button:

Google Form stores each lead in a Google Sheet

How to get this link (2 minutes)
  1. In forms.google.com make a form with 3 short-answer questions: Name, Phone, Email.
  2. Click ⋮ (3 dots, top right) → Get pre-filled link.
  3. Type exactly name in the Name box, phone in Phone, email in Email.
  4. Click Get link → Copy link and paste it above.
  5. Keep other questions optional. Responses appear in the form's Responses → Sheets.

In the Google Form add a 4th short-answer question and type service in it while making the pre-filled link.
